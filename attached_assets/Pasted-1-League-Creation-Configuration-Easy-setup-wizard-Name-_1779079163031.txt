1. League Creation & Configuration
Easy setup wizard: Name, type (scratch/handicap), team size, season dates/length, schedule generation (auto round-robin or manual).
Handicap settings (base, %, individual/team, recalc rules).
Point system presets + custom options.
Rules configuration or selection from USBC-style templates (averages, absentee, playoffs, eligibility, fees, etc.).
Fees/prize fund setup, public vs. private leagues, divisions (optional).
Roles: League admin/secretary, treasurer, team captains.
2. Discovery, Joining & Onboarding
Search/browse leagues (location, type, skill level, open spots, handicap/scratch).
View details, rules summary, current standings/schedule.
Join as individual or create/join a team + invite friends.
Approval flows or open registration.
New bowler onboarding (average establishment, rules explanation, etiquette).
3. Team & Roster Management
Create/edit teams, manage rosters and substitute lists.
Player profiles with averages, history, and stats.
4. Scheduling & Operations
Auto or manual schedule generation.
Lane assignments and notifications.
Support for postponements, pre-bowling, and position rounds.
Split-season and playoff configuration (various formats: total pins, best-of, stepladder, etc.).
5. Score Entry, Averages & Handicaps
Manual score entry or integration/import from bowling center scoring systems.
Automatic calculation of averages, handicaps (per game/series), team totals, and points.
Handling of absentees (absentee score logic), substitutes, vacancies, and tardy players.
Real-time or post-session updates.
6. Standings, Stats & Reporting
Real-time or updated standings (team points, records).
Rich individual/team stats (average, high game/series, handicap scores, trends, most improved).
Leaderboards and historical archives.
Printable recaps, financial reports, and award eligibility tracking.
7. Communication & Engagement
Push notifications (upcoming league, lane assignments, standings updates).
In-app announcements, chat, or highlights sharing.
Social/community features to enhance the “league experience.”
8. Admin & Moderation Tools
Dashboards for secretaries/treasurers.
Rule enforcement/logging and dispute/protest handling.
Participation tracking and eligibility reports.
Multi-league support (for centers or power users).
9. Additional/Advanced
Mobile-first experience (bowlers view everything on phone).
Analytics and personal improvement tracking.
Support for USBC sanctioning/export if desired.
Hybrid/virtual elements or unique formats (no-tap, etc.).
Strong data accuracy and edge-case handling (new bowlers, few games bowled, etc.).
